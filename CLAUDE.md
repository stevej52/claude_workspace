## Talking with the repo owner

- The owner often dictates messages, so they contain filler words such as "uh" and "um". Ignore those words; they carry no meaning and need no acknowledgement.
- The owner pauses while dictating to find the right word, so a message may arrive halting, repetitive, or cut off mid-sentence. Read for the intent. When a message seems unfinished, wait for the rest before acting rather than guessing.
